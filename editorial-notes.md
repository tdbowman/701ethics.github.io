# Editorial Notes ⚠️

This book was rebuilt for Fall 2026 from the previous reading list plus the current revision notes, then checked citation by citation. This page records what was corrected, what was replaced, and what you should check before citing. It is here rather than hidden in a commit message because a book about information ethics should show its own sources.

## How this book was made

Chapter summaries were written from the source texts themselves wherever the text could be obtained, and every 2025–2026 citation was verified against a published record. No quotation appears in this book unless it was read in the source, with a locator. Where a text could not be obtained, the entry says so on its face rather than guessing.

Portions of the drafting were assisted by AI tools, with all content reviewed and approved by Dr. Bowman. That workflow is disclosed here for the same reason it is required of students: in a course about information ethics, provenance is not optional.

If you find an error, a dead link, or a summary that misreads its source, tell Dr. Bowman. The text always wins over the summary.
